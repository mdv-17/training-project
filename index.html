Running and strength planner

A personal training project that brings running, strength workouts and recovery into one plan.

Project

I’m developing a web app with a training calendar, an exercise library, a workout builder and progress tracking. Strength suggestions fit around running sessions and account for recent training and recovery.

This site provides project and privacy information for my Garmin Connect Developer Program application.

API use

  • Activity API: import completed runs and strength sessions, including recorded sets, repetitions and weights where available.
  • Health API: use sleep, resting heart rate and HRV trends to adjust upcoming sessions.
  • Training API: let participants edit suggested workouts and send them to Garmin Connect for a compatible watch.

The requested integration depends on Garmin approval and participants’ consent to the relevant data processing.

Beta plans

I plan to test the app free of charge with a small group of invited Garmin users. Their feedback will guide further invitations. There are no current plans to charge for access.

Invitations will begin after API access, participant consent and the remaining privacy arrangements are in place.
