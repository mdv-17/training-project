Running and strength planner

A personal training project that brings running, strength workouts and recovery into one plan.

Project

I’m developing a web app with a training calendar, an exercise library, a workout builder and progress tracking. Strength suggestions fit around running sessions and account for recent training and recovery.

Features

  • Plan running, gym and home workouts in one calendar.
  • Build and edit strength workouts from an exercise library.
  • Track completed sessions and recorded sets, repetitions and weights where available.
  • Adjust upcoming sessions using recent training, sleep, resting heart rate and HRV trends.

A planned Garmin connection would import activity and recovery data and send selected workouts to Garmin Connect for a compatible watch. Workout publishing is not available yet.

Beta plans

I plan to test the app free of charge with a small group of invited Garmin users. Their feedback will guide further invitations. There are no current plans to charge for access.

The beta is not open yet. Development is focused on the Garmin connection and preparing the app for invited testers.
