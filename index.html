Training and recovery

A web application in development that brings running, strength training and recovery information into one place.

About the project

The application is being developed to use Garmin training and health data, with each user's permission, to support training decisions.

  • Review running activities and recovery information.
  • Keep Garmin Run Coach workouts as the running plan.
  • Recommend strength workouts that fit around running.
  • Explore sending structured workouts to compatible Garmin devices.

Current status

The application is still in development. Testing is planned with a small group of invited users. Garmin data connections and workout synchronization are not available on this information site.
